I'm Dereck

I trained as a physics teacher in Cuba, in Spanish, on the Isla de la Juventud. After that I taught for twenty years — first in Zimbabwe, then in England.

I am a registered mental health nurse. I work on a psychiatric intensive care unit. I live in Corby. I still tutor Physics, and I build revision sites and small-business websites because the job is the same: make the next step obvious.
